Playground 02 — In progress

A fully-connected network, fitting a curve in front of you.

Pick an objective function — start with a parabola — generate noisy 2D samples from it, then watch a configurable neural net close the gap between its prediction and the data. Zoom in on the curve to see individual ReLU units bend into place, and watch dropout simplify the model in real time.

  • 01 Objective function picker (parabola, sine, step, custom) with adjustable parameters and Gaussian noise
  • 02 Side-by-side model comparison against the true distribution, tracked by live MSE
  • 03 Fully-connected network hyperparameters — depth, hidden width, learning rate, dropout rate
  • 04 Interactive zoom that reveals ReLU hinge points moving during training
  • 05 Dropout visualization showing the effective network simplify as units are silenced
