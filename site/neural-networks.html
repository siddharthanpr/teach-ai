Fitting a curve to data

Pick an objective function to generate noisy data, then pick a model to fit it with — a neural network, or a parametric family (line, polynomial, sinusoid, …) learning its own coefficients by gradient descent. Choose a model that doesn't match the objective to see how well one function family can approximate another.
